How to play. Swipe each card left or right, or tap a button. Every choice moves four things: Faith 🙏, Flock 👥 (your people), Finances 💰 and Family 🏠. Watch out for Naija news: NEPA, fuel scarcity, ASUU strikes and more can hit at any time. If faith or family runs dry, or your flock or finances hit zero or grow beyond what you can steward, the journey pauses with a lesson. As you grow you'll unlock Heroes of Faith: true milestones from the lives of Nigerian ministers, gospel artists and missionaries. Collect them all in the Hall of Faith.

Anonymous play stats (never your name) help us improve the game.
